### Added (v3)
- **Moment pages** `/m/[segmentId]`: shared Moments land on a branded page (vertical clip, quote, full session, Ask bar) instead of a bare `.mp4` (Phase 16)
- **Cloudinary share cards**: Open Graph images for Moments and sessions, generated by Cloudinary from the video frame (Phase 16)
- **Learner Insights**: knowledge gaps, most asked questions, Moments that travel (Phase 14)
- **Study Packs**: summary, concepts, clip-backed quiz, "Session in 60 seconds" reel (Phase 13)
- **Answer Reels**: every answer as one video stitched from cited moments across sessions (Phase 12)
- **Gemini AI layer** with a model fallback chain (Phase 11)
- Core loop, Phases 01–09: Studio upload, Watch, webhook ingest, Find, Library, Moments, grounded Ask, hardening
